# Frame design system

Frame borrows the quiet confidence of a cinematheque's programme: warm paper-colored text, ink-dark surfaces for evening browsing, generous editorial type and untinted film artwork. Lime is reserved for selection and meaningful actions. The product is a browsing tool, so navigation and feedback stay predictable.

## Tokens
- Canvas #111512; raised surface #1B211C; secondary surface #242C25.
- Primary ink #F5F3E9; muted ink #A6B0A4; rule #333D33.
- Accent #D5ED86 with #18220C on accent; error #FFB4A6; amber #EBC888.
- DM Sans for controls and content; Instrument Serif for editorial headlines.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48. Portrait cards have 12px corners.

## Composition
A compact Frame wordmark and useful search action begin Discover. A serif invitation leads into one featured real film, followed by a popular/top-rated selector and a virtualized poster grid. Search foregrounds its field. Saved films show a quieter personal collection. Detail screens preserve the same artwork and place rating, synopsis, and save action within easy reach.

## Behavior
Tabs on phones, a navigation rail at wide widths; 48dp controls. Preserve cards while refreshing, retry inline at the point of failure, and expose offline status without blocking the app. Fullscreen errors only when there is no usable content. Poster placeholders remain legible when artwork is unavailable. Reduce-motion setting disables skeleton animation. System back, safe areas, accessible labels, and text scaling are supported.

## Source integrity
Ratings and summaries come from the selected live catalog. Default collection is explicitly identified as animated films. Poster ownership and rating provenance are credited in About. No invented ratings, availability claims, or pretend accounts.
